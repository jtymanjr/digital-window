Digital Window

Your screen becomes a pane of glass. The front camera follows your eyes and the scene is redrawn from exactly where you sit, so things behind the glass shift like real scenery and a few objects float in front of it.

Best with one eye closed (or a bit of distance), the browser in fullscreen, and a minute spent on Calibrate.

Calibrate

1 · Screen size

Hold a bank card against the screen and drag the slider until the dashed box is exactly as wide as the card (85.6 mm).

85.6 mm

2 · Camera position

About 1 cm on a fullscreen laptop. In a windowed browser add the height of the tabs and toolbar.

3 · Distance

Sit at a distance you know, enter it, and press Set. This tunes the camera's field of view so depth is right.
